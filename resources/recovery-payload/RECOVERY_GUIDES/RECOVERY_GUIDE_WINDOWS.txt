Welcome to Dero Vault Rescue & Migration for Windows 10 and 11.
This backup drive is 100% self-contained and allows you to restore your files
without internet access on any replacement or new computer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: DIRECT FILE ACCESS (ZERO SOFTWARE REQUIRED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All your protected files are stored in standard, non-proprietary formats:
1. Open Windows File Explorer.
2. Navigate to this USB drive.
3. Open the folder:
   DERO_VAULT_BACKUP\CURRENT\
4. You will see your folders (e.g., Documents, Desktop, Pictures) exactly as
   they were organized on your original computer.
5. You can copy and paste any files directly to your new computer.
6. If you need previous revisions of edited files, open:
   DERO_VAULT_BACKUP\VERSIONS\

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: GUIDED RECOVERY VIA DERO VAULT SETUP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you want Dero Vault to automatically restore your files, map your user
profile, verify cryptographic hashes, and resume automatic protection:
1. In the root of this USB drive, double-click:
   Rescue-Windows.bat
2. Or run the verified offline installer located at:
   DERO_VAULT_BACKUP\RECOVERY\INSTALLERS\WINDOWS_X64\DeroVault-Setup.exe
3. Follow the setup wizard. Dero Vault will recognize this drive automatically
   and open the "Rescue & Migration" workspace.
4. Review your backed-up folders, select target destination directories,
   and click "Restore My Office".
5. All restored files are verified using SHA-256 to ensure byte-perfect integrity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY & PRIVACY GUARANTEES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Source Safe: Your backup files on this drive will NEVER be deleted or modified.
- Non-Destructive: Conflicting existing files on the destination computer are
  never overwritten; they are preserved with a " (restored)" suffix.
- 100% Local: No internet connection is required. No telemetry or cloud syncing.
