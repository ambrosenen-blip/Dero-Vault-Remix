Welcome to Dero Vault Rescue & Migration for macOS (Monterey, Ventura, Sonoma, Sequoia).
This backup drive is 100% self-contained and allows you to restore your files
without internet access on any replacement Apple Silicon (arm64) or Intel (x86_64) Mac.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 1: DIRECT FILE ACCESS (ZERO SOFTWARE REQUIRED)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All your protected files are stored in standard, non-proprietary formats:
1. Open Finder on your Mac.
2. Select the USB drive in the sidebar.
3. Open the folder:
   DERO_VAULT_BACKUP/CURRENT/
4. You will see your folders (e.g., Documents, Desktop, Pictures) exactly as
   they were organized on your original computer.
5. Drag and drop any files or folders to your Mac.
6. If you need previous revisions of edited files, open:
   DERO_VAULT_BACKUP/VERSIONS/

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHOD 2: NATIVE LAUNCHER SCRIPT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the root of this USB drive, double-click or run:
   Rescue-macOS.command
2. Or in Terminal, run:
   sh "/Volumes/<DriveName>/Rescue-macOS.command"
3. The interactive recovery portal will guide you through restoring your files
   and automatically sanitize any Windows-specific filename characters (e.g.,
   colons, asterisks, or reserved names) for macOS compatibility.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SAFETY & PRIVACY GUARANTEES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Source Safe: Your backup files on this drive will NEVER be deleted or modified.
- Non-Destructive: Conflicting existing files on the destination computer are
  never overwritten; they are preserved with a " (restored)" suffix.
- 100% Local: No internet connection is required. No telemetry or cloud syncing.
